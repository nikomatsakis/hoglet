# Authority

[Requirements](requirements.md) record the product outcomes and their sources.
[Decisions](decisions.md) record choices affecting the system and their rationale.

The engineer's stated requirements, including local reading and editing with
GitHub-established service access, are accepted. The TypeScript, concurrent-edit,
login-acquisition, and dual-transport decisions are also accepted from explicit
engineer selections. Inferred guarantees and other proposed choices remain
draft for review. An accepted record is part of the goal design;
it is not implementation or verification evidence.
